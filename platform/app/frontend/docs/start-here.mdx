## 1. Brainstorm your project

You have about 6 weeks, which is enough for one substantial project. Choose something you've wanted to build for a while, like a 3D printer, wearable synth head, etc.

Remember, you'll be demoing your project at Maker Faire! Projects that people can _experience_ are often the most memorable.

But more importantly... your project must work!! Settle on an idea that _you can build and have working in 1.5 months._

We love crazy projects, but scope creep does too! We recommend building something straightforward that _works_, then adding more features to it.

If you don't have an idea yet, here are some prompts:

1. Create something you can wear
2. Something that people can physically interact with
3. A display that doesn't use a screen

We encourage you to build one super cool project over these next 6 weeks. But you may build multiple projects if you finish your first one early, are waiting on parts, or are still trying to learn!

All your projects will count for XP, but you need at least one main project (this is the one that qualifies you for Fallen!)

We'll fund all your projects, but each project you make must be built! It needs to be physical for you to demo it at Maker Faire!

_Unsure if your idea is fit for Fallen? Share it in [#fallen](https://hackclub.enterprise.slack.com/archives/C0BNNV3ASNP) for feedback!_

If you'd like to continue your project from another Hack Club program, you can transfer it through this form.

## 2. Pick your team

When you sign up, you'll choose between Team Lulu and Team Sami. In case you haven't already, sign up [here](/)!

Pick the team that fits your making style (you can change your pick later!):

### Lulu

- Each week you journal 5 or more days, you earn +30 XP
- The week starts once you make your first journal
- E.g. journal 3h a day for 5 days a week, qualify by December 7th!

### Sami

- Each week you journal every day, your XP multiplier goes up by +0.15x, to a maximum of 1.75x
- The week starts once you make your first journal
- You have 3 freezes, used each time you miss a day. After all 3 are used your multiplier resets to 1x.
- E.g. journal 1h every weekday, 4h per day on the weekend, and qualify in ~90 hours

> Not sure if you can complete this? [Read a note from us.](/docs/a-note)

### LUMI (Lulu + Sami) of the Week

Every Saturday at 11:00 AM, the team with the highest combined XP from its top 50 contributors will be crowned LUMI. Each contributor on the winning team earns +10 XP!

## 3. Journal your progress

We want to see your process. This ensures we're funding _real projects_ (unfortunately, we've been tricked before)!

So, we came up with a plan...

Each time you work on your project:

1. Join the 24/7 huddle in [#fallen](https://hackclub.enterprise.slack.com/archives/C0BNNV3ASNP) (optional, highly recommended)!
   - Upon joining, Lulu & Sami will ask what your goals are for this session. When you leave they'll check up on your goals and tell you how long you worked!
2. **Post a journal in [#fallen-journals](https://hackclub.enterprise.slack.com/archives/C0C9B7FHY3A) or on [fallen.hackclub.com](https://fallen.hackclub.com/) with what you did + how many hours it took**
   - If you joined the huddle, use the time Lulu & Sami gave you!
   - A journal should contain:
     - What you did
     - What worked? What didn't?
     - The time you spent in the session
     - Pictures of what you worked on
   - A journal SHOULDN'T contain:
     - 0 images
     - AI generated text
     - Only what worked. We want to hear about your process too!
   - We _aren't_ focusing on your writing skills! We want you to write something you'd be proud to look back on, and something that's readable to others

Read more on journaling and a model journal in [On Journaling](/docs/journaling).

_This might sound like a lot to remember, but Lulu & Sami have got your back! They'll remind you on Slack if you forget something._

## 4. Evolve your character & come to Shenzhen!

As you earn XP, your Sami or Lulu evolves! The baseline is 8 XP per hour journaled, meaning 4 XP per half hour, etc.

To come to Shenzhen, you need to first:

- Reach 1000 XP (max evolution!)
  - You shouldn't need to worry about hour deflations, deflations are rare. They occur only if your time estimate seems way too high for the progress made. We'll reach out if this happens - it's unlikely to.
- Have a finished & working project

Keep on journaling until you've completed these two stages...

**1. Finished your digital design:** Awesome! Read through our [Submission Guidelines](/docs/submitting), then submit your design to Design Submission (coming soon!)

For real... please check the [Submission Guidelines](/docs/submitting) _before_ submitting! It'll help us fund your project faster :)

**2. Finished building your project IRL:** Review the [Submission Guidelines](/docs/submitting) before submitting your Build Submission (coming soon!)

You are one step away from qualifying, and we're SO excited to see what you've made!
