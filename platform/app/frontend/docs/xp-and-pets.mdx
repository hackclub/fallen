Your journals earn XP for the hours in them. Your current XP, team and bonuses are on Home.

## Your pet

The pet you picked starts as an egg and hatches with your first journal. As your XP grows it changes form, stage by stage. The final form waits for your project to be approved.

Hover over your pet to see how far it is from its next form. You can hide it or call it back in Settings.

## Teams

Your pet is also your team, Lulu or Sami, and each earns a bonus its own way.

- **Lulu** gets bonus XP on Monday for journaling on enough days the week before, Monday to Sunday.
- **Sami** builds a multiplier on every journal for each week without a missed day. A missed day spends a streak freeze; with none left, the multiplier goes back to 1x. The streak starts with your first journal.

Everyone on the team that leads the week gets bonus XP on Monday.
