Once your Design Submission is approved, we'll send you a grant card to buy what your project needs to get built!

## Receiving your funding

We'll email you a grant card from our Hack Club financial platform, [HCB](https://hcb.hackclub.com).

You can use this card:

1. **Online:** Enter the card details provided
2. **In-Person:** Add it to a mobile wallet (Apple/Google Pay)

This card counts as a credit card, so be sure to select that when checking out!

## How much you'll get

Project funding is based on the **_project's quality, polish, and complexity. Your project should justify the funding it requires._**

It is **NOT based on how long you spent, nor your XP amount**!

We're a non-profit with limited funding, so **_please only request the funding amount you need_** (in your `bom.csv`).

## In your BOM

✅ Good to include:

- Buying the minimum amount of each component needed for your project
- Buying the bare minimum tools you need
  - e.g. A $20 soldering iron is more than enough!
- Paying for the cheapest manufacturing options
  - e.g. Using HASL instead of ENIG for your PCB (both are types of PCB surface finish, ENIG has gold and is more expensive!)

🙅 Do not include:

- Extra parts!
- Overly expensive tools
- Modules/kits for parts you're supposed to design
  - e.g. An ESP32 devboard → Make your own ESP32 based PCB
- Things unrelated or unnecessary for your project
