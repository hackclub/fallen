Traveling to China can be expensive! To help you get to Shenzhen, we're offering travel stipends.

For every XP you earn beyond the 1000 XP needed to qualify, you'll get $1 USD in travel stipends (so 100 XP = $100 USD) _Reminder: XP doesn't fully depend on your time spent. Your team's bonuses and multipliers count too!_

Given that there's not much time until Fallen, **you can keep earning stipends after Fallen's IRL event.**

<p class="rounded-[4px] bg-white px-4 py-3 font-montserrat text-lg font-semibold text-slate-ink">
  You have until April 10th, 2027 to earn travel stipends!
</p>

> _All travel stipends will be sent as a reimbursement after the IRL event, ongoing until April 24th._
>
> _If you require these funds before purchasing your flight, please email us at [fallen@hackclub.com](mailto:fallen@hackclub.com)_

## Need-based stipends

On top of regular travel stipends, we've set aside some funds to offer to fallers on a case-by-case basis.

These stipends are saved for fallers who couldn't attend without extra financial support, each are up to a max of $1000 USD. When reviewing applications, we'll also check out your project!

**To apply, you need at least 500 XP.** That way we know you're on track to come!

These stipends are in very limited quantity, so we won't be able to support everyone who applies, but we will read every application, so don't be deterred from applying!
