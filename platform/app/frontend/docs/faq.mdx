### What is a "Lulu or Sami"?

When you sign up, you'll choose between Team Lulu and Team Sami.

They each provide different XP bonuses or multipliers based on your consistency! Choose your team on the website, and read how each one works in [Start Here](/docs/start-here).

### Why Shenzhen, China?

We'll be demoing our projects at Maker Faire Shenzhen, in the manufacturing capital of the world. While we're there, we'll visit local hardware spots like the world's largest electronics market.

### What do I build?

One large hardware project (think a month and a half of dedicated work!). Keep in mind you'll be demoing this project in Shenzhen!

### How many people can attend?

We estimate ~30 attendees, but we have no upper limit. If you qualify, you can attend Fallen IRL!

### Can I make multiple projects?

Yes, but pick one main project to focus on! You can also build smaller projects on the side to get started with hardware, make trinkets to hand out, or for fun!

### Can I follow a tutorial?

You can learn from tutorials, but your project must be your own.

### How long do I need to spend on my project?

Your character levels up with the hours you put in, and you qualify once it reaches its final form. Journaling every day builds a streak that speeds up its progress, so qualifying takes about 80-125 hours depending on how well you keep your streak & your character's bonus!

### What if I can't go to Shenzhen?

You can still be a faller! Level up your character to earn prizes like 3D printers and CNC mills, or exclusive Fallen merch.

### Are my flights covered?

Read about our travel stipends in [Travel Stipends & Visas](/docs/travel-stipends)!

### How is my project funded?

It's based on what your project needs! Read more in [Project Funding](/docs/project-funding).

### Can I transfer my project?

If you'd like to continue your project from another Hack Club program, you can transfer it through [this form](/transfers).

### Can I work in a team?

Yes! After creating a project on the platform, you'll have an option to invite teammates. You each will journal your own sessions, and the XP of a journal goes to whoever wrote it.

Qualification requirements stay the same (1000 XP per person)!
