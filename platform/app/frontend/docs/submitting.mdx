Your project needs to be shipped before you can submit it!

_"what the heck is a shipped project ??"_

> _A shipped project is a project you've shared with the world. Shipping is just as important as building: it's what makes your project real. It's no longer just a folder on your computer._

A good Fallen ship clearly shows off your project, includes all files nicely organized, and is publicly available (a GitHub repo is the most common choice!).

**There are two types of submissions:**

1. Design Submission: you've finished the digital design of your project! Once it's approved, we'll send you project funding to build it!
2. Build Submission: you've built your project IRL and it works!! Once it's approved, you've qualified for Fallen!! :DD

## Your `README.md`

_This is what people first see when viewing your project, so make it clear and awesome :)_

<Checklist>

**It should include:**

- What your project is and how to use it
- Why you made it
- Screenshots of your project's:
  - Full 3D assembly
  - PCB
  - Schematic or wiring diagram
  - 3D render (optional)
- Anything else you'd like (e.g. a poster, banner or pinout diagram)

**For a build submission, also:**

- Link your demo video
- Add images of the final product

</Checklist>

## Your repository

<Checklist>

**It should include:**

- `STEP Files (.step)` for each part you've modeled
- The source project assembly file (.f3d, .FCStd, or a link to Onshape), including electronics
- The PCB source files
  - For KiCad that includes at least `.kicad_pro`, `.kicad_sch`, `.kicad_pcb`, `.kicad_prl` and `gerbers.zip`
- Your firmware (for a design submission, it's fine if it's untested)
- A Bill of Materials (BOM) as a `.csv`, with a link to each item (even if you already own the item - mention it!).
- Any other part of your project (libraries/references/etc.)

**For a build submission, also:**

- Make sure your source files are up to date

</Checklist>

Not everything above may apply to your project (maybe yours has no PCB), and that's completely okay! If you're not sure whether to include something, include it, or ask in [#fallen](https://hackclub.enterprise.slack.com/archives/C0C7Y736SF7)!

_Lastly, your project should be original, designed and documented by you:_ not an AI agent, not someone else, just you 🫵. Don't just copy a guide 1-to-1. Add your own twist and make it unique!
