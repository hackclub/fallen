Your project lives as a thread in the journals channel on Slack, and every journal is a reply in it, posted as you.

Journals you write on your project page post to that thread. Replies you write in the thread show up on your project page too.

## Writing one

Open your project and press **Write**. Say what you did this session, how long it took, and add photos.

## Hours

Each journal has the hours you worked. If one is missing them, reply to the bot in your Slack thread with how long you worked, or edit the journal on your project page.

## Slack

You need Slack connected to create projects and write journals. You can connect or disconnect it in Settings.
