title: '1. Ship it'
order: 2
---

Shipping means putting your project out in the world, not just in a folder on your computer.

1. Put everything in a public GitHub repo: your KiCad project, your CAD files and your firmware.
2. Write a `README.md` that says what your Tamagotchi is and how to use it, with screenshots of the schematic, the PCB
   and the full 3D assembly.

The full checklist is in [Submission Guidelines](/docs/submitting).
