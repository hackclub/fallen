- **Change the shape.** A cat, a cloud, a ghost, a slice of toast. The board outline can be anything.
- **Add LEDs** for a mood light, or one that blinks when it wants attention.
- **Draw your own art** on the silkscreen with **File → Image Converter**.
- **Give it a personality** in the firmware: new animations, mini-games, or a pet that gets grumpy if you ignore it.

Share it in [#fallen](https://hackclub.enterprise.slack.com/archives/C0BNNV3ASNP)! I want to see what you made.
