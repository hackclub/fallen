## Who can join?

Any teenager aged 13 to 18 (inclusive)!

## Is this legit?

Yes! [Hack Club](https://hackclub.com) is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, and we've run programs like
[Fallout](https://fallout.hackclub.com) and [Outpost](https://outpost.hackclub.com) before.

## Is this really free? What's the catch?

No catch! Fallen funds your project and the in-person event.

## Why Shenzhen, China?

We'll be demoing our projects at Maker Faire Shenzhen, in the manufacturing capital of the world. While we're there,
we'll visit local hardware spots like the world's largest electronics market.

## What do I build?

One large hardware project (think a month and a half of dedicated work!). Keep in mind you'll be demoing this project
in Shenzhen!

## Can I make multiple projects?

Yes, but pick one main project to focus on! You can also build smaller projects on the side to get started with
hardware, make trinkets to hand out, or for fun!

## Can I follow a tutorial?

You can learn from tutorials, but your project must be your own.

## Can I work in teams?

Yes! Each teammate needs their own clear part of the project, and journals their own hours to level up their own
character.

## How long do I need to spend on my project?

Your character levels up with the hours you put in, and you qualify once it reaches its final form. Journaling every
day builds a streak that speeds up its progress, so qualifying takes about 80-120 hours depending on how well you keep
your streak & your character's bonus!

## What if I can't go to Shenzhen?

You can still be a faller! Level up your character to earn prizes like 3D printers and CNC mills, or ***exclusive Fallen merch***.

## I've got more questions...

Ask in [#fallen-help](https://hackclub.enterprise.slack.com/archives/C0C9JQEPFL6) on Slack or email us at
[fallen@hackclub.com](mailto:fallen@hackclub.com)!
