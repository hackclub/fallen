# The plan

Rough. Numbers will change.

## The pitch

Build one big project in ~6 weeks and level up your character to attend a hardware adventure in China.

## How participation works

- Journals are posted in the Slack channel.
- A constant huddle roughly tracks time, which participants reference in their journal.
- Joining the huddle: post a goal brief for the session.
- Leaving the huddle: prompted with "did you accomplish {task}?" yes / no / partially, plus an optional why not.
- Netflix-style "are you still there?" check-ins during the huddle.

## XP, streaks and the pet

- Everyone has a desktop pet. If the pet has evolved to its final stage by the deadline, you have qualified.
- 8 XP per hour journaled, raised by Sami's streak multiplier or topped up by Lulu's weekly bonus.
- Streaks give XP multipliers.
- Everyone starts with 3 streak freezes.
- All XP earned after qualifying goes towards travel.
- Qualification takes roughly 80 to 125 hours depending on streak use.

## Two tracks

- In-person attendees travel to Shenzhen.
- Everyone else picks from a shop of high-value prizes in tiers by final XP: FDM or resin printer, laser cutter,
  microscope, and so on. This track funds the experience and needs the most push.

## Principles

- Project funding is quality based. Qualification is hour based.
- Could run on Fillout forms + Slack + a very simple site showing everyone's stats and progress.
